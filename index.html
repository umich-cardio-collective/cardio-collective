About The Club

The Cardio Collective was founded at the University of Michigan out of a passion for promoting cardiovascular health across the Ann Arbor community and beyond. Through education, service, action, and outreach, the organization was established to unite undergraduate students interested in cardiology and medicine around a shared commitment to making a lasting impact on community wellness.

Because cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of death—affecting countless individuals and families worldwide—The Cardio Collective strives to combat this epidemic locally through student-led educational initiatives and community engagement. Recognizing the profound impact cardiovascular disease has on patients and their loved ones, the organization remains dedicated to spreading awareness of heart health, wellness, and preventive care, and welcomes students of all backgrounds to join its mission.
