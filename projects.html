Our Projects & Initiatives

Discover our hands-on workshops, clinical outreach, educational opportunities, and public health initiatives designed for students and the Ann Arbor community.

Key Initiatives

CAN (Cardiovascular Awareness)

We travel to local schools to teach elementary students about the importance of cardiovascular health. By starting early, we aim to build lifelong healthy habits in the youth of our community.

Keynote Speaker Series

We host medical professionals and experts to share their clinical experiences and research with our members. Join us for our upcoming event featuring a guest doctor in early November!

Community Volunteering

Our members actively participate in local health initiatives and community service. We proudly partner with organizations like the Gift of Life Foundation to support organ and tissue donation awareness.

CPR Training

Free, accessible hands-on CPR and AED training sessions offered to students and community members across campus. We equip participants with life-saving skills and confidence to respond during cardiovascular emergencies.

Suture Clinics

Interactive hands-on clinical workshops teaching basic surgical knot-tying and suturing techniques. Guided by medical professionals and upperclassmen, students gain valuable exposure to surgical fundamentals.

Survival Flight Tours

Exclusive guided educational tours of Michigan Medicine's Survival Flight facilities. Members get a behind-the-scenes look at critical care air transport, life-support equipment, and conversations with flight nurses and paramedics.

Heart Friendly Blog

A student-driven digital publication translating complex cardiovascular research, preventive health habits, and clinical topics into accessible, engaging articles for the general public.
